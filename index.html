Why ReMind

  1. Understand it

    Two short videos a week explain what is changing in your brain, and what helps.

  2. Practise it

    Quick games on WhatsApp. Reply by text, emoji or voice note.

  3. Live it

    A private worksheet, then a small group session with a psychologist.

A week that fits into your chai break

One topic a week, in small daily moments. About 5 to 10 minutes a day, all on WhatsApp.

  1. Day 1

    Make sense of it

    A short video on what is changing, and why

    5 to 6 min
  2. Day 2

    Play with it

    A quick, playful game

    2 to 5 min
  3. Day 3

    Find what helps

    A video with one simple strategy

    5 to 6 min
  4. Day 4

    Try it out

    A game to practise the strategy

    2 to 5 min
  5. Day 5

    Make it yours

    A private worksheet for your own day

    Your own time
  6. Day 6

    Pause

    Rest, or catch up on anything you missed

    Rest day
  7. Day 7

    Come together

    A small group session led by your psychologist

    45 min, live

Six weeks. Six parts of you.

Each week takes one part of everyday life and gives you a few simple ways to handle it.

  1. Week 1

    Memory

    “Where did I keep my keys?”

    Simple systems that make remembering easier.

  2. Week 2

    Decisions

    “Why is choosing dinner so hard?”

    Make choices without feeling overwhelmed.

  3. Week 3

    Habits

    “Starting again on Monday… again.”

    Understand your habits and build ones that stick.

  4. Week 4

    Stress

    “When everything feels urgent.”

    Practical ways to respond to everyday pressure.

  5. Week 5

    Boundaries

    “Saying no without the guilt.”

    Put your needs into words, kindly and clearly.

  6. Week 6

    Sleep and body

    “Wide awake at 3 a.m.”

    Small routines for better sleep and wellbeing.
